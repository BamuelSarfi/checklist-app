Privacy Policy

How personal and workplace information is collected, used, stored, and protected.

1 Information Collected

This application may collect:

Employee name or identifier
Form entries and record submissions
Submission timestamps and audit information
Generated PDF records and related metadata
2 How Information Is Used
Support daily operational record keeping
Generate printable and downloadable records
Track completion and submission status
Support internal review and compliance needs
3 Storage and Security

Records are stored on the application server and protected through access controls. Sensitive files, including employee data, should not be publicly accessible.

4 Sharing

Information is not sold or shared externally except where required for internal business use, legal compliance, or authorised system operation.

5 Retention

Records are retained for operational and compliance purposes. Retention periods may vary based on business requirements and applicable policy.

6 Your Responsibilities
Keep login details secure
Only access records you are authorised to view
Report any suspected security issue immediately

For privacy questions or concerns, contact the system administrator or your manager.
